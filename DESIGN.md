## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an unapologetic, high-energy Neo-Brutalism aesthetic engineered specifically for modern Indonesian personal finance management. The brand personality is optimistic, proactive, transparent, and electric—demystifying money management for digitally savvy millennials and Gen Z. By ditching corporate austerity in favor of tactile sticker culture, comic-book punchiness, and razor-sharp clarity, the experience transforms everyday budgeting and AI financial coaching into an engaging daily ritual.

The visual style merges stark graphic comic contrasts with structured product utility:
- **Neo-Brutalist Tactility:** Crisp solid strokes (2px to 3.5px), zero-blur directional offset drop shadows, and high-impact surface color blocking.
- **Vibrant Pop Utility:** Hyper-saturated accent fills anchored firmly against a warm, grounded cream foundation.
- **Indonesian Tone & Vernacular:** Friendly, empowering conversational cues delivered through clean visual stickers and structured conversational components.

## Colors
The palette leverages high-voltage primaries balanced against a tactile cream canvas and deep ink accents:

- **Primary (`#FFE600` - Pop Electric Yellow):** Used for primary call-to-actions, core AI insight callouts, and key highlight banners. Emphasizes urgency and instant clarity without alarmist friction.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF` - Vibrant Cyan):** Represents automated actions, category badges, connected e-wallets/bank feeds, and interactive secondary triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#FF4365` - Vibrant Coral Pink):** Highlights expense alerts, critical warnings, negative cashflow trends, and playful sticker accents.
- **Accent Green (`#00FF66` - Punchy Neon Green):** Dedicated indicator for income, positive balances, safe spending limits, and successful transactions.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FFFDF8` - Tactile Cream):** Off-white base canvas preventing visual fatigue caused by pure white screens under saturated neons.
- **Ink Black (`#121212` - Deep Obsidian):** The structural backbone used for heavy borders (2px–3.5px), crisp typography, iconography, and zero-blur offset drop shadows.

## Typography
The typography pairing contrasts the expressive, architectural geometry of Space Grotesk for prominent headlines, labels, and financial figures with the clarity and human warmth of Plus Jakarta Sans for reading and conversational flows:

- **Numerical Hierarchy & Currency:** Space Grotesk is set to tabular numbers (`tnum`) for all Rupiah (`Rp`) currency displays, transaction lists, and financial analytics to ensure precise visual alignment down decimals and zeros.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Badges, interactive tags, and micro-headers default to Space Grotesk Bold uppercase with deliberate positive letter tracking, producing an editorial, sticker-like character.
- **Body Text:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures long conversational AI explanations and transaction histories remain readable without visual vibration.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a responsive fluid-grid structure engineered for rapid financial skimming:

- **Mobile (default, up to 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with a `1rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` gutters. Elements stack vertically, prioritizing balance displays, daily action prompts, and transaction feeds.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and gutters. Supports two-column modular arrangements for summary metric cards alongside AI recommendation feeds.
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px`, centered with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates multi-pane split workflows (e.g., chat sidebar, multi-account overview, deep transaction analytics).
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Component internals adhere to standard multiples of `0.5rem` (8px), maintaining structured, breathable separation between hard-bordered elements.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with Neo-Brutalist principles, depth is purely physical and mechanical rather than atmospheric:

- **Hard Drop Shadows:** Layers convey elevation through crisp, zero-blur directional offset shadows in `#121212`. The default card elevation features `4px 4px 0px #121212`.
- **Active & Pressed States:** Interactive elements physically depress upon interaction, shifting down and right via `transform: translate(2px, 2px)` while reducing shadow offset to `2px 2px 0px #121212`, or flattening completely (`0px 0px 0px #121212`) on full press.
- **Modal & Floating Elevation:** Floating sheets, sticky navigation bars, and dialog modals utilize an emphasized offset of `6px 6px 0px #121212` with a `3px` solid stroke.
- **Absence of Gradients and Blurs:** No frosted glass or soft gradient drop shadows are permitted. Visual separation relies strictly on solid fills, outlines, and offset planes.

## Shapes
To counterbalance the heavy visual weight of hard borders and offset shadows, elements use rounded geometry:

- **Border Radii:** Outer container cards, interactive panels, and primary banners use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) or `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) corners to provide a soft, tactile, neo-industrial silhouette.
- **Pills for Badges & Stickers:** Status tags, currency indicators, and categorization chips utilize full pill contours (`rounded-full` / 9999px) paired with rigid 2px solid borders.
- **Stroke Weights:** A consistent boundary rule applies across all surfaces:
  - Default cards and input fields: `2px solid #121212`.
  - Buttons, major summary cards, and focused elements: `2.5px` to `3px solid #121212`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Pop Electric Yellow (`#FFE600`), 2.5px solid `#121212` border, `4px 4px 0px #121212` shadow, font Space Grotesk Bold. Hover triggers a slight expansion or color shift to Vibrant Cyan (`#00F0FF`); active state translates `2px 2px` with a `2px 2px 0px` shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** White or Cream background, 2.5px solid `#121212` border, identical shadow metrics.
- **Danger Button:** Vibrant Coral Pink (`#FF4365`) background with crisp black text and borders.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Styled as graphic stickers. Filled with vibrant tints (`#00F0FF`, `#FFE600`, `#00FF66`, `#FF4365`), bounded by a `2px solid #121212` outline, and paired with uppercase Space Grotesk micro-labels. Occasional subtle tilt angles (-1.5deg to +1.5deg) on dashboard summary badges reinforce the sticker metaphor.

### Cards & Financial Containers
- Solid Cream (`#FFFDF8`) or clean white background, surrounded by `2.5px solid #121212` and a `4px 4px 0px #121212` shadow.
- Card headers feature clear separation through a `2px solid #121212` horizontal divider or high-contrast background fill.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Thick `2px solid #121212` border with `0.5rem` radius. Inactive background is pure white; active/focus state gains a vibrant `#00F0FF` or `#FFE600` `3px 3px 0px #121212` offset outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-drawn geometric aesthetic. Square with `3px` roundedness for checkboxes, circles for radios, each enclosed in `2px solid #121212`. Checked state fills with `#00FF66` or `#FFE600` containing a thick black checkmark.

### AI Assistant Callouts (Smart Financial Coach)
- Conversational cards wrapped in a `#FFE600` or `#00F0FF` solid stroke with a comic-style prompt badge ("💡 DOMPI TIPS" or "ANALISIS DOMPI"). Text set in Plus Jakarta Sans with bold contextual highlights for Rupiah targets and savings percentages.

### Transaction Lists
- Row items separated by crisp `2px solid #121212` bottom borders or individual micro-cards. Left avatars feature category stickers with neon fills; right values display bold tabular Space Grotesk numbers (+Rp untuk pemasukan in `#00FF66`, -Rp untuk pengeluaran in `#FF4365` or `#121212`).
